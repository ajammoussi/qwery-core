AI Writing Tropes — Short Version
Avoid AI-sounding vocabulary
Overused adverbs: “quietly”, “deeply”, “fundamentally”, “remarkably”
Corporate/LLM buzzwords: “delve”, “leverage”, “robust”, “streamline”, “harness”
Grandiose nouns: “tapestry”, “landscape”, “ecosystem”, “paradigm”, “synergy”
Fancy replacements for “is”: “serves as”, “stands as”, “represents”
Avoid formulaic sentence patterns
“It’s not X, it’s Y”
“Not X. Not Y. Just Z.”
“The problem? …”
Repeating the same sentence opening
Excessive rule-of-three structures
Empty transitions: “Importantly”, “It’s worth noting”
Fake analytical endings: “highlighting its importance”
Fake ranges: “from innovation to transformation”
Avoid artificial tone
Manufactured suspense:
“Here’s the thing”
“Here’s the kicker”
Patronizing teaching voice:
“Think of it as…”
“Let’s break this down”
Futurist hype:
“Imagine a world where…”
Fake honesty/vulnerability:
“And yes, if we’re being honest…”
Stakes inflation:
“This changes everything”
Vague authority:
“Experts say…”
Made-up analytical labels:
“acceleration trap”
“supervision paradox”
Avoid structural tells
Too many ultra-short dramatic paragraphs
Disguised listicles:
“The first… The second…”
Constant summaries/repetition
Reusing the same metaphor endlessly
Historical analogy spam
Repeating one idea in many forms
Explicit conclusion markers:
“In conclusion”
“To sum up”
Avoid formatting tells
Excessive em dashes
Bold-first bullet lists
Fancy Unicode arrows/quotes
Core principle

One or two of these is fine. The problem is density and repetition. Write:

specific instead of grandiose
direct instead of theatrical
varied instead of formulaic
concise instead of padded
human instead of “polished AI”
